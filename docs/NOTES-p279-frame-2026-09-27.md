## 0. Owner decisions (Michael, 2026-09-27)

1. **Approved words:** program name `Long Ride + Strength`, under the goal `Go longer`.
2. **Four-ride option: yes.** An optional switch, `Easy ride on Day 5` (words still to be confirmed with
   Michael). When it is off, the **Day 5 endurance ride** comes out. The book's reasoning: easy riding is
   cut before hard work (p134, "the cut comes out of the EASY miles"); p281 names Monday and Saturday as
   this program's priorities (Day 1 sweet spot, Day 6 long ride); and p131's keystone sessions are "the
   ones that require you to be in the most recovered state to perform". Of the three easy rides (Days 2,
   5, 6), Day 6 is a priority and Day 5 sits the day before it, so Day 5 is the one removed.
3. **Room to grow: rides get longer, offered and accepted, never automatic.**
   - **When:** at the book's timing. This program's long ride every fourth week (p281). Base's long ride
     every 1 to 2 weeks (p281). Midweek endurance rides once a month (p281, Base's "1-month cycles").
   - **How much:** at most 5% of the week's easy minutes. p148: *"aiming to change each of these by less
     than 10 percent per week, though ideally 5 percent is as high as I will usually go"*.
   - **Ceiling:** a ride never leaves its level's printed range (p239: level 1 endurance 60 to 100 min;
     level 2, 2.5 to 3.5 hours; level 3, 3.5 to 5 hours).
   - **How it reaches the rider:** the step-up is offered and the rider accepts it, the same pattern as
     an FTP change. It is never applied on its own.
   - ⚠️ **Ripple:** this names Base's timing too. Base today is built with rides that never lengthen
     (`printedWeekOnly: true`, Michael 2026-09-13, `WORKORDER-ride-strength-2026-09-13.md` §3). That
     ruling and its back-annotation are not changed by this note (docs-only instruction); the build
     session must mark it.
4. **Level 3 rides: build item.** The app's level-2 ride cap is lifted for this program (§2 item 1).

## 1. How the week differs from Base (p279 against p278)

| | Base (p278) | p279 |
|---|---|---|
| Lifting days | 1, 2, 4 + plyo warm-up day 3 | same |
| Rides, Standard | 7 (two on days 3 and 5) | **5, one a day** on days 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 |
| Ride levels, Standard | 1, with sweet spot "1-2" on day 1 and endurance 2 on day 6 | sweet spot **"2 to 3"**, endurance **2**, VO2 **2**, endurance **2**, endurance **3**/LSR |

| Rides, Deload | 5, all level 1 | 5, all level 1: sweet spot, endurance, sprint, endurance, endurance |

The one-session convention (two workouts in one box are one session unless marked "(separate sessions)",
p245, p253, p263, p269; exception printed on p258) joins **nothing on p279**: every box holds one ride.
On p278 it joins Standard days 3 and 5. Whether the Base frame builds those two days as one session or two
was not traced this session.

## 2. What the frame will need that Base's frame does not

**Build items (decided):**
1. **Lift the level-2 ride cap for this program (decision 4).** `RIDE_LEVEL_CEILING = 2`
   (`frames.ts:1929`, Michael 2026-08-27) clamps every ride through `clampRideLevel`; today p279's day 1
   "level 2 to 3" and day 6 "level 3" would both build at level 2. The frame needs its own ceiling of 3.
   The text shown with the cap (`RIDE_LEVEL_CEILING_CITE`, `frames.ts:1931-1934`) says level 3 sweet spot
   "is prescribed in none of his cycling programs"; p279 day 1 prescribes it, so that line changes too.
2. **The four-ride switch (decision 2).** Base's version is `fewerRidesDropsSlot` (day 2). This frame drops
   day 5's endurance ride. The switch words are not confirmed yet.
3. **Offered ride step-ups (decision 3).** New: nothing offers a longer ride today. Needs the timing per
   ride, the 5% limit, the p239 range as the ceiling, and an accept step like the FTP one.
4. **Deload lifting that swaps intent.** Base's deload only removes rows; p279's turns ME rows into SKILL
   or DE. The All Rounder's deload already does this, so the row format holds it.
5. **A deload ride of a different type.** Deload day 3 is a sprint ride where Standard day 3 is VO2. The
   two columns are written separately, so it fits; it reuses Base's sprint slot (standing start left out,
   Michael 2026-09-13).
6. **"2 x HYP" superset on day 4.** The adjacent-rows pattern exists (`frames.ts:821`, p244 and p274).
7. **Card and ride-screen words.** Name and goal approved (decision 1). The blurb and rides screen still
   need Michael's words: the level 2 and 3 endurance rides run 2.5 to 5 hours (p239).
8. **Plumbing any new frame needs** (Base work order §2): a new `FrameId`, a `FRAMES` entry, a
   `RATE_ANCHOR` entry, the card in `NonRaceBuilder.tsx`, the step list in `wizard-steps.ts`. The Base
   template, once it lands, may change this list.
9. **Same as Base, no new work:** three lifting days, plyo warm-up day 3, day 7 rest, rides only, no
   overhead press named (tested lifts bench, squat, deadlift), the carry row's approved wording, p280's
   working-max note ("a few more percentage points", no number).

**Answered from the page:**
- **"LSR" on day 6 = the week's long ride.** p275 uses "the weekend LSR" for the long slow session and says
  it "can be a hike, a long ride"; p281 calls this program's day 6 ride "the long ride" and names Saturday
  a priority. It is built as the day 6 endurance ride at level 3; "/LSR" adds no other content.
- **"Focused push/hinge lower" = a lower-body push and a lower-body hinge.** p222-223 name the two
  categories "Focused push lower/quads" and "Focused hinge lower/hamstrings"; the row's words are those two
  names with "lower" written once. The upper-body focused push is written "focused push" with no "lower"
  (p279 day 1). This is a reading of the category names, not a sentence on the page.
- **Priority days** are days 1 and 6 (p281). Decision 2 uses them; nothing else in the frame needs them.

**The eight points the page could not answer — settled by Michael, 2026-09-27:**
1. **Day 1 "level 2 to 3": the rider picks**, the way the book leaves p258's "level 1 to 2" to the runner
   (p259).
2. **"DR" on deload day 2 follows the standard week's row in the same position**, read as a print slip.
   Standard day 2's first row is "1 x ME: Primary hinge lower (rotate with primary push)", so the deload
   row is built as **ME**. The deload week keeps the heavy hinge.
3. **Deload day 1's rows without "Accessory:" follow the standard rows in the same positions.** They are
   built as "1 x DE: Accessory: primary pull" and "1 x DE: Accessory: secondary push". Neither is a
   competition lift. The "ME Upper" and "ME Lower" headings stay as the standard week prints them.
4. **The "Full" heading follows the standard week.** Both columns of p279 print "Full", so it stays
   "Full". The difference was only against p278's "DE: Full".
5. **"Every fourth week" = weeks 4, 8 and 12, counted from the plan's start.**
6. **Rides with intervals count only their easy minutes** toward the 5% growth limit (Michael's reading of
   p148: the limit is on easy minutes). The interval minutes are not counted.
7. **The four-ride switch also removes the day 5 ride in deload weeks**, as Base's day 2 switch works in
   both columns (`fewerRidesDropsSlot`, read "in both columns").
8. **Lift progression: the same rule as Base** (`RATE_ANCHOR.cycling_base`, `perWeek: 0`, lifts move only
   when earned).

**Nothing on p279 is left open.** Still to confirm with Michael: the switch words `Easy ride on Day 5`, and
the card blurb and rides-screen words (build item 7).

## 3. Who it is for (p280)

Riders who have run Base for at least 4 weeks and want "steady output/climbing/longer distance";
"events where the terrain (climbing and/or technical) is what dictates the race more than the
competitors"; "muscular endurance and durability for multiple longer efforts close to threshold". p280
also names Base and this program as the best framework of the three for powerlifters (big three one or
two times a week, DE and ME). p281 adds that it can lead into a long-course triathlon.
