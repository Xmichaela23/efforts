# DRAFT — a how-to for every movement a plan can print (2026-09-18)

Status: **words for approval, nothing built.** One row per movement as the athlete sees it on the row. Where a movement
has a station version and a home version, each gets its own row, because they are done differently.

## (a) What a plan can print

Counted by building plans, not by reading the catalogue: both frames (all_rounder, cycling_base) × 10 kits (none declared,
commercial gym, home barbell, full home, dumbbells only, dumbbells + bar + bands, kettlebell only, bands + bar, barbell
minimum, bodyweight) × tested and untested × every focus chip, then every option of every pick cell one at a time, 2,924
builds, 16 weeks with the taper. Every row's name and every name on its swap list was collected. Rows the week adds on its
own (core, the muscle floor) are switched off in the code today and print nothing.

- **125 names** reach a row or a swap list; after merging spellings of one movement (`rdl` / `romanian deadlift`, `t bar row` /
  `T-bar Row`, `kb swings` / `Kettlebell Swing` and so on) and splitting station from home versions, that is **126 how-tos**: 10 approved, 116 new.
- **Already have one (the approved 14 entries, 10 printable movements):** Back Extension (floor, barbell over the feet),
  Back Extension (GHD bench), Weighted Reverse Hyper, Reverse Hyper (flat bench), Chest-Supported Row (incline dumbbells),
  Bent-Over Dumbbell Rear Delt Fly, Chest-Supported Rear Delt Fly, Flat-Bench Dumbbell Pullover, Concentration Curl,
  Dumbbell Leg Curl. `calf raise` has one but no plan prints it now.
- **Missing: 116**, below. The station versions of the approved home movements (Leg Curl, Preacher Curl, Pullover Machine,
  Rear Delt Machine, Chest-Supported Row, Reverse Hyperextension) are in the 116: today an athlete with the machine sees
  no how-to for them.
- **DB Pullover** uses the approved pullover words unchanged; it is the same movement under its own name.

## (b, c) The words

Source column: the page the steps were written from, opened and read 2026-09-18. Every row now does what its page
does; where a page did not match the row's own equipment it was replaced with one that does. **18 rows had no page** in ExRx, ACE or NSCA (ExRx keeps several behind a paywall and those were not used). Those 18

### Plyometrics

| movement | how-to | source |
|---|---|---|
| A-Skip | Skip forward on the balls of your feet. On each skip, lift one knee until the thigh is level with your hip, then bring the foot straight back down under your hip. | B-Skip | Skip forward as for an A-skip. At the top of each knee lift, straighten the leg out in front of you. Pull the foot down and back so it brushes the ground backwards as it lands under you. | Bounding | Jog a few steps, then push off one foot and lift the other knee until the thigh is level with your hip. Stay in the air as long as you can and land on that front foot. | Stiff-Legged Run | Run forward with your legs straight, starting slowly and building speed. Keep your upper body upright and do not lean back. As each foot lands, pull it backwards along the ground. | [Adam Hodges, PhD / TrainingPeaks, "8 Running Drills to Improve Your Running Form" (Straight Leg Run)](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/blog/drills-for-proper-running-form/) · Viada p227 names it |
| Single-Leg Hops | Stand on one foot beside a line or a low cone, hip and knee slightly bent. Hop sideways over it and back, landing on the ball of the foot and taking off again as soon as you land. | Rebound Jumps | Stand with feet hip-width apart. Jump straight up, land on the balls of both feet with the knees slightly bent, and jump again as soon as you land. Spend as little time on the ground as you can. | [ExRx, "Vertical Jumps"](https://exrx.net/Plyometrics/VerticalJumps) |

| Lunge Hops | Stand in a split stance with one foot forward and your knees slightly bent. Dip down, then jump straight up and switch legs in the air. | Ickey Shuffle | Stand at one side of an agility ladder, facing down it. Step into the first square with the inside foot, then the outside foot, then step out to the side of the next square with the first foot. Repeat the in, in, out pattern into each square, so you move up the ladder from side to side. Keep your feet low to the ground. | [Matt Toupalik, ACE CPT / Cactus Athletics, "Ickey Shuffle Ladder Progression #1"](https://cactusathletics.com/ickey-shuffle-ladder-progression-1/) |
| Hopscotch | Stand at the bottom of an agility ladder on your left foot. Hop on that foot through the first three squares, then jump both feet out to either side of the ladder. Hop your right foot back in and hop on it through the next three squares, then jump both feet out again. Keep going to the end, landing only in the squares and never on the rungs. | [Amber Sayer, MS, CPT / Marathon Handbook, "10 Agility Ladder Drills"](https://marathonhandbook.com/agility-ladder-drills/) · Viada p227 names it |

## (d) How the how-to becomes part of the movement

What changes for the athlete: every row has the (i) beside its name, on every kit, the main lifts included. Today it
appears only when the plan swapped a machine movement for a home one.

1. **The table is keyed by the movement the athlete does, not the one the book names.** Today `EXECUTION_HOW_TO`
   (`_shared/strength-grid/grid.ts:607`) keys the home words under the machine's name ("preacher curl" holds the
   concentration-curl words). It becomes one entry per name the row shows: "concentration curl" gets those words,
   "preacher curl" gets the station words. Where one name is done differently by kit (chest fly, rear delt fly,
   external rotation, lateral raise, cable curls, chest-supported row) the entry keeps the per-kit form the table
   already supports.
2. **`executionHowTo` (`grid.ts:629`) loses its substitute-only gate** (the "athlete has the station → nothing" check) and
   becomes: find the name the row shows (`executionName`), return its words.
3. **`rowHowTo` (`standing-plan/compose.ts:2424`)** drops its "main lift → nothing" line, so Back Squat, Bench Press,
   Deadlift, Overhead Press and the rest carry theirs. The four places the composer writes `how_to` (`compose.ts:1777,
   1920, 3817, 3977`), the two in `materialize-plan/index.ts` (`:2861`, `:3295`) and the logger's swap (`StrengthLogger.tsx:5246`)
   already call the same function and need no change.
4. **Blocks built before the change** carry no words on their rows. They get them on the next rebuild: the restate already
   compares `how_to` row by row (`standing-plan/restate.ts:300`). No change on the phone; the (i) and the sheet
   (`StrengthLogger.tsx:5006`, `:6909`) already show whatever the row carries.
5. **A check that stays green only while every printable movement has words:** the sweep above, as a test beside the
   golden blocks. The three golden files gain "· how-to" on most rows and get rewritten.
6. Ledger: one row in `docs/STATE-SOURCES.md` pointing at this file for the sources, the same as the GHD back extension.
