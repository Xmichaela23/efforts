      [warning ] Friday heavy legs, Saturday long run. A very hard session of 14+ work sets may cut performance in other training a lot for twenty-four hours, and noticeably for up to seventy-two hours. An easier session of 6 to 8 work sets may cause only small performance drops for twenty-four hours, with few problems forty-eight hours later.  (Viada p86)
